# Rider help centre

Made-up help articles for the made-up bike-share service used throughout
this course. Lesson 068 searches these notes to answer rider questions.

## Prices and passes

A single ride costs KSh 50 for the first 30 minutes. After that, every extra
15 minutes costs KSh 20, charged automatically when you dock.

A day pass costs KSh 300 and covers unlimited rides of up to 45 minutes each
for 24 hours from the moment you buy it.

A monthly pass costs KSh 2,500. Students with a valid student ID get the
monthly pass for KSh 1,500.

## Paying with M-Pesa

You can pay with M-Pesa or with a debit or credit card. To use M-Pesa, choose
it in the app's Wallet screen and approve the prompt on your phone.

If you are charged twice for the same ride, the duplicate charge is reversed
automatically within 3 working days. You do not need to contact us.

Refunds for any other reason are sent back to the M-Pesa number or card you
paid with, and take up to 7 working days to arrive.

## Full or empty docks

If you arrive at a station and every dock is full, tap "Dock full" in the app.
You get 15 extra minutes free to ride to the nearest station with space.

If a station has no bikes, the app's map shows live bike counts for every
station, updated every minute.

## Late returns and lost bikes

Bikes must be docked by midnight. A bike kept out overnight is charged a late
fee of KSh 500 on top of the ride price.

A bike that has not been docked for 48 hours is treated as lost, and the
account holder is charged KSh 25,000 unless it is reported stolen to the
police and to us within that time.

## Reporting a broken bike

If a bike is damaged, tap the spanner icon in the app and scan the bike's QR
code, or dock it and press the red button on the dock twice.

A ride on a bike you report as broken within the first 5 minutes is free, and
the bike is locked until a mechanic checks it.

## Accounts and safety

You must be at least 16 years old to open an account. Riders under 18 need a
parent or guardian to approve the account in the app.

Helmets are not provided with the bikes, but we strongly recommend wearing
one. Lights on every bike switch on automatically when you start pedalling.

## Contacting support

Our support team answers calls and chats from 7am to 9pm, every day of the
week, including public holidays.

The support phone number is 0800 720 720 and calls are free. You can also
email help@example-bikes.co.ke and we reply within one working day.
